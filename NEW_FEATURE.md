Custom puzzle creation
High effort
Let users build their own grids and clues, then share a link to their puzzle. Start with a simple editor and validation rules before attempting collaborative editing.











Weekly quests
Medium effort
Challenges like completing three medium puzzles, solving without hints, or finishing a hard puzzle. Rewards could be achievement badges, profile decorations, or new themes.

Leaderboards and competitions
Medium–high effort
Daily rankings, weekly challenges, and friend competitions. You'll need reliable player identities and server-validated completion times to make rankings meaningful.

Customizable Gameplay
Allow users to configure the experience.
- Light, dark, and system themes (mainly the system version)
- Keyboard controls and customizable shortcuts
- Sound effects and reduced animations
- Adjustable text and cell sizes
- Optional timer and mistake counter
Why it fits: These settings help the game work well across desktop and mobile without changing the puzzle-generation architecture.
