Level music (audio/music/level-<world>-<1..4>.mp3, added 2026-10-10): a calm song for every campaign level, each one
different, picked to fit its world. All CC0 from https://opengameart.org/content/<page> (each page checked). For the
game each one got a seamless loop (2026-10-11): the point where the song matches itself best (drums and harmony),
lined up on the beat with a 60 ms crossfade; the file plays once, then loops between the points in level-loops.json.
2.5 minutes at most, levelled to the same, quieter loudness. Titles are as listed on OpenGameArt. Rainy City 1-3
(jazz), Neon Arcade 1-3 (chiptune), Acorn Woods 4 and Candy Hills 2 were swapped on 2026-10-11 (the user's request).
Garden 1 was swapped on 2026-10-13 for a song that loops cleanly; ten songs got better loops that day (a seam that
matches, the whole song end to start, or a song that fades out running its last 4 s into its first 4 s).
