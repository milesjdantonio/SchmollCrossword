      Settings

      Saved in this browser

      World

      The whole look: background, board, sounds, mascot and win animations. Hover a world to see it move.

      Mode

      Every world has a day and a night look: Notebook turns into a blueprint, Deep Space trades its sun for a black hole, the reef glows in the dark. This world is always dark.

      Sound

      Every sound is made in code, live, in your browser. Each world has its own.

      Effects

      Lite uses fewer particles and a calmer background, for older devices. (Your device's "reduce motion" setting turns animations off.)

      Default difficulty

      Easy uses the most common words; Hard adds rarer ones.

      Like OVOID or ADZE. The daily never uses them, so it's the same for everyone.

      Reset

      Can't be undone.

      About & credits

      Schmoll Crossword is free, with no ads. A tiny crossword, three fresh ones every day, plus a bigger Challenge.
