Schmoll Crossword word list (words.txt)
=======================================

words.txt is a combination of two word lists. It keeps only the words that appear in BOTH:

1. SCOWL (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists), size 50
   by Kevin Atkinson, http://wordlist.aspell.net/
   Version 2020.12.07, files english-words.* and american-words.* at levels 10-50.
   License: see SCOWL-Copyright.txt (permissive; that notice must be kept with this file).

2. Spread the Word(list), answers scored 50 or higher
   by Brooke Husic and Enrique Henestroza Anguiano, https://www.spreadthewordlist.com/
   Downloaded 2026-10-07 (spreadthewordlist_caps.txt).
   License: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International
   (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

Changes made:
- Kept only entries made of the letters a-z (no spaces, hyphens, apostrophes, accents or capitals).
- Kept only words found in both lists above, and dropped the Spread the Word(list) scores.
- Converted to lowercase, one word per line, sorted alphabetically.
- (At load time, the game also skips every word in blocked-words.txt.)
